# LinkedIn article image: vision prompt

Taken from the visionary lines in `PRD/`:

- "Have you recently replaced coding with prompting? Next: replace clicks with no clicks."
- From a chat window to a live URL, without the intimidating worlds of Git, terminals and DNS.
- Your pages live on your own corner of the edge network: owned by you, not rented.
- Project memory: a site you can come back to and edit from any future conversation.
- The chat window turns into a publishing terminal; a conversation turns into a place on the web.

## Master prompt

A luminous, cinematic editorial illustration of a conversation becoming a place on the internet.
On the left, a single softly glowing chat speech bubble floats in calm deep-indigo twilight. From it,
ribbons of warm light and faint lines of code unfurl like folding paper, assembling mid-air into a
crisp, bright, beautifully designed web page that stands upright like an open doorway on the right.
Through that doorway lies a sunlit horizon: many small luminous web pages drift upward like lanterns
above a vast, quiet planetary network of fine glowing threads and edge nodes, a personal corner of the
internet that belongs to its maker. No cursor, no buttons, no keyboard, no hands: the page appears as if
spoken into being. Warm amber and Cloudflare-like sunset orange light meeting deep blue night, volumetric
glow, gentle depth of field, refined premium tech-magazine cover aesthetic, wide 16:9 composition with
calm empty negative space in the upper third. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no
logos, no watermark.

## Variations (one per image)

Each image keeps the master prompt and adds one accent (see `generate.py` for the exact list):
the doorway, a bridge of light, paper lanterns, an origami unfolding, a lighthouse, a constellation map,
seeds sprouting into pages, a calm sunrise over the edge network, a minimalist isometric version and a
retro-futurist poster version.

Output: 1920x1088 generated, center-cropped to 1920x1080 (16:9).

## Batch 2: sowing and other "opening up" metaphors (images 11-20)

The door in batch 1 made the direction ambiguous (are messages going in or coming out?). Batch 2 drops it:
every scene moves left to right, outward, toward the horizon, and the chat bubbles are blank.

Sower prompt (11-15): a farmer strides left to right across a field at dawn, casting a sweeping arc of glowing
chat bubbles like seeds. The furrows are a dreamy, glowing internetwork; where bubbles landed, small web
pages sprout, small near the farmer and in full bloom toward the horizon.

Other metaphors: a sluice gate releasing a stream of bubbles into a valley of web pages (16), paper boats with
speech-bubble sails leaving a canal lock (17), a bud bursting open and releasing bubble pollen (18), a spring
thaw freeing a stream (19), and dandelion seeds carrying bubbles (20). Exact wording is in `generate.py` (`BATCH2`).
